Executive Summary

Generic ACCUs closed the week at $38.15 after opening Monday at $37.65, while premium methods kept clear spreads to generic. The biggest move came in Environmental Plantings, which moved from $51.00 on Monday to $51.55 by Friday.

Trade volume for the week came to 830.5k tCO2e across the supplied trade log. Activity was concentrated in generic and HIR-linked segments, while premium methods remained thinner but held their pricing separation.

Week's Key Driver

Policy timing still dominated sentiment, with the market waiting for the next concrete method and Safeguard signal.

Market Condition

Generic ACCUs stayed rangebound, while Environmental Plantings moved higher and closed at a clear premium.

Market Context & Outlook

Safeguard & Compliance

The modest uplift in generic ACCU pricing this week reflects ongoing Safeguard baseline compliance positioning. Entities reporting at mid-year are undertaking portfolio optimization to align surplus/deficit positions ahead of 30 June reporting. The concentration of trading in generic and no-AD segments confirms this is compliance-driven rather than speculative activity.

Source: RepuTex ACCUsmart assessments; Jarden trade log

Method Development Pipeline

The persistent premium for co-benefit methods (Plantation Forestry +4.5%, SFM + ICB +27.5%, Environmental Plantings +35.6%) underscores sustained buyer demand for abatement paired with biodiversity, habitat, or ecosystem health outcomes. This structural premium is expected to persist as Australian organizations increasingly incorporate nature-positive criteria into procurement mandates.

Source: DCCEEW method approval tracker; buyer procurement feedback

Market Outlook

Generic ACCUs are likely to remain rangebound around $37.50-$38.00 until the next policy catalyst lands. Premium methods should stay supported, but recent EP volatility argues for more caution there than in Plantation or SFM + ICB.

Source: RepuTex, CORE Markets, DCCEEW
