Trade Activity & Liquidity

Weekly trade activity totalled 242,300 tCO₂e across four method types. Generic ACCUs dominated at 130,000 tCO₂e (53.7% of volume), followed by Human Induced Regeneration at 75,000 tCO₂e (31.0%). Generic (No AD) contributed 32,300 tCO₂e (13.3%) and Environmental Plantings 5,000 tCO₂e (2.1%).

The largest individual trade was a 100,000 tCO₂e Generic put option (Sep-26 delivery, A$35.00) transacted on 5 June. Spot market activity was broadly distributed through the week, with HIR spot trades and Generic (No AD) lots ranging from 10,000 to 40,000 tCO₂e.

Market Context & Outlook

DCCEEW — IFLM Method Development

DCCEEW provided an update on development of the Integrated Farm and Land Management method. Public consultation on the draft method closed on 9 March 2026, with 64 submissions received from carbon service providers, peak bodies, NGOs, government, universities and individuals.

The key design issues still being worked through include discount settings, project baselines, measurement approaches and overall method complexity. This points to continued development work before the method is finalised, rather than an immediate near-term supply impact.

Source: DCCEEW ACCU Scheme IFLM method update

Market Consolidation — May Context

CORE Markets' May ACCU update noted that the market consolidated after April's rally, with spot ACCUs briefly testing A$38 before closing May at A$37.55. The report also noted ongoing discussion around Safeguard-held inventory and near-term market balance as key watchpoints for price direction.

Source: CORE Markets ACCU Market Monthly Update — May 2026

Weekly Market Read
